# Cross-channel resubmissions

A third of providers resubmit on a second channel before hearing back (fax, then portal). Status-check calls are mixed into the call log. Naive counting double counts requests and shortens response times.

- Reporting year: 2026
- True requests: 304
- Seed: 202

Files: fhir_pas.ndjson, portal_export.csv, fax_ocr.txt, call_center_log.csv, appeals.csv. truth.json is the answer key and is never read by the reconciler.
